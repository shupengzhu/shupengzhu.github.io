About AtmoNex

We help businesses, research organizations, and professional clients turn scientific questions into reproducible computational workflows, decision-ready analyses, and practical technical solutions. Engagements are typically project-based and tailored to the scope, data, modeling requirements, and deliverables of each client.

Services

Our work spans atmospheric and environmental modeling, scientific computing, geospatial analysis, AI-assisted analytics, and custom technical development.

Atmospheric & Air Quality Modeling

WRF, CMAQ, CESM and related modeling workflows for meteorology, emissions, atmospheric chemistry, transport, air quality, and scenario analysis.

Climate & Environmental Analysis

Quantitative analysis of climate, environmental exposure, environmental risk, and scenario-based impacts using scientific datasets and reproducible methods.

CFD & Indoor Environment

Computational fluid dynamics and related simulation support for airflow, transport, ventilation, and indoor or built-environment applications.

Remote Sensing & GIS

Geospatial data processing, satellite and remote-sensing analysis, spatial matching, mapping, and GIS-based analytical workflows.

AI & Data Analytics

Machine-learning, statistical, and data-engineering solutions for scientific prediction, emulation, data fusion, diagnostics, and automated analysis.

Custom Scientific Software

Development and integration of research software, model-processing tools, automation pipelines, dashboards, and specialized computational applications.
